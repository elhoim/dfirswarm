### Added: a skill budget check, run by the seal and by a suite, and a `method_only` list for skills that name nothing of their pack

`scripts/skill-budgets.py` holds every pack's skills to the size budgets of the skill-loading design, estimated as bytes over 4.245 of the whole file: a skill is at most 800 tokens (a warning from 800; `pack.sh seal` refuses one over 1,000, before it writes anything), a router at most 400, a skill file at most 300 lines, a skill's line in `skills/INDEX.md` at most 40 tokens, `needs` at most two deep (a loop is a finding), and a skill together with every skill its `needs` reach, across the pack's `depends`, at most 1,900 tokens. A chain within 150 tokens of that limit warns, naming the skills it is made of; and sealing a base pack names the base skill whose growth would push which dependants over (or says which dependants its growth already put over), the way a grown base skill failed other packs' chain tests before. The seal prints the advice as `budget:` lines. `tests/skill-budgets.test.sh` runs the program over the shipped packs and proves each rule on small packs built to break it, the chain counted through `depends`, the warning bands, the seal's refusal and that a refusal leaves the pack as it was.

The shipped packs already have 129 findings (31 skills over 1,000 tokens, 15 index entries over 40, 29 skills whose `needs` reach past two, 54 chains over 1,900), tracked in `tests/skill-budgets.golden` as the link findings are: `pack kind skill ceiling`, sorted, so a listed skill cannot grow past its ceiling and a line goes when its skill is brought within the budget (a stale line fails). `scripts/skill-budgets.py golden` writes the list. No skill file changes; the split of the skills over budget is the next step.

### Fixed: the link lint counted a skill's id as a naming of a program

`tests/pack-links.test.sh` read a skill's `id:` and `needs:` lines when it looked for the programs a skill names, so `id: aws/cloudtrail` made the `aws` program of the cloud pack count as referenced. Those two lines are left out, and `aws` is now in the cloud pack's `unreferenced_ok` with its reason (an operator's acquisition capability that no skill or tool of the pack runs).

### Added: `method_only` in `pack.json`

The counterpart of `unreferenced_ok` for the rule that a skill names something of its own pack: `method_only: [{"path": "skills/reporting/for-regulators.md", "why": "..."}]` lists a skill that names no tool, program or package of its pack on purpose. The lint reads it, fails a path that is no skill of the pack, an entry with no `why`, and a path listed twice, and reports an entry whose skill does name something of its pack after all. Nine skills were the exceptions and are now listed with their reasons: `reporting/citations` and `reporting/disagreement` (base), `google/workspace-access` and `logs/what-exists` (cloud), `ios/unified-logs` (mobile; it runs the macOS pack's `unified_log`), `encryptor/destroyed-backups`, `exfil/before-encryption` and `reporting/for-regulators` (ransomware) and `execution/overview` (Windows). `docs/packs.md` (section 8, "Links" and the new "Skill size") says both. Packs: base, cloud, mobile, ransomware and Windows (manifest only).
