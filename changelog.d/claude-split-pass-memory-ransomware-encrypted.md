### Changed: the memory-forensics, ransomware-response and encrypted-containers skills are split into leaves within the size budget

The skill budget split pass (`docs/packs.md`, "Skill size") for three packs. Every skill that was over a budget is now a decision leaf plus second-level leaves it points to with "only if X", and no leaf is over 800 tokens (the largest is 799), no index entry over 40, no `needs` chain over 1,900 tokens or depth over 2, no file over 300 lines. 46 lines leave `tests/skill-budgets.golden` (memory-forensics 22, ransomware-response 17, encrypted-containers 7); nothing is added to it.

- **memory-forensics** (9 skills become 19). `triage/volatility` (1,784 tokens) is now the offline rule and the record, with `triage/volatility-symbols`, `triage/volatility-plugins` and `triage/volatility-catalogue` behind it; `triage/what-you-have` points to `triage/container-details`, `triage/no-framework` to `triage/carve-signatures` and `triage/paging-files`, `processes/injection` to `processes/preserve-regions`, `network/state` to `network/timestamps-dns`, `credentials/material` to `credentials/key-schedules` and `acquire/images` to `acquire/avml-and-preserve`. The Volatility offline rule, the "fetch nothing" rule for a missing symbol table, the dated 2.28.2 facts and the secret and no-hash rules are carried over word for word; the one sentence that changed for another reason is in `triage/volatility-catalogue`, which no longer says the base `memory-windows` recipe retries without `--offline` (on main it runs offline at every step).
- **ransomware-response** (7 skills become 17). `scope/first-hour` points to `scope/triage-tools` and `scope/phase-timeline`; `encryptor/traces` to `encryptor/windows-evidence` and `encryptor/samples-and-notes`; `encryptor/destroyed-backups` to `encryptor/backup-infrastructure`; `exfil/before-encryption` to `exfil/measure-and-claims`; `identify/family` to `identify/samples-and-decryptors`; `recovery/what-is-possible` to `recovery/routes-and-decryptors`; `reporting/for-regulators` to `reporting/notification-register` and `reporting/conclusions-and-controls`. The notification clocks are still named by their trigger, the skills state no deadline and no applicability, and no law of one country is in them.
- **encrypted-containers** (5 skills become 11). `identify/headers` points to `identify/by-format`, `volumes/bitlocker` to `volumes/bitlocker-opening`, `volumes/luks-filevault` to `volumes/filevault-apfs`, `archives/protected` to `archives/formats`, `keys/where-they-hide` to `keys/recovery-key-scan` and `keys/other-sources`. The password-recovery gate still gives no method.

Every old skill id is kept. The `needs` edges that only pointed at another note (including those that reached into the base pack's `evidence/verify`, `filesystem/encrypted` and `reporting/citations`) are now text ("read X for how"), so a chain is a skill and at most one other. No Shows / Does not show / Record / Sensitive output / bounded-negative / corroboration sentence was dropped: the sentences were moved, four `Does not show` lines that covered several topics were divided between the leaves that now carry each topic, and the sentences that were reworded are listed in the pull request. New suites `tests/pack-memory-skills.test.ts`, `tests/pack-ransomware-skills.test.ts` and `tests/pack-encrypted-skills.test.ts` hold the size and shape rules, the pointers between leaves, the ids, a register of the review's rule sentences (`tests/pack-split-rules/`), the secret and notification rules, and the claims the review removed. `docs/packs.md` and `README.md` carry the new skill counts (199 in all, the base pack's 25 included). Skills only: no tool, requires or image changed.
