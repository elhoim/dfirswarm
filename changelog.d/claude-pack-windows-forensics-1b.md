### Changed: the Windows pack's 24 skills say what each record shows, what it does not and what corroborates it

Every skill of `windows-forensics` is rewritten against the tools as PR 1a fixed them and against the worker prompt's rules. The overclaims are gone: adjacent RIDs as a sign of an attacker's account, the Recycle Bin SID as who deleted a file, ShellBags and UserAssist as a person's action, `$LogFile` as an independent wall clock, a gap in record ids as a count of missing events, today's timezone bias applied to a past date, `ProfileList` as a full session history, the absence of a persistence entry as intent, event 5145 as a file read, and an empty channel as a cleared one. In their place each skill names the observation, what it shows, what it does not and what corroborates it, words a negative as "No evidence of X was found in <objects>, <interval>" with the coverage conditions (status, parse errors, retention, configuration), keeps tool names and Windows build facts to what the pack's code and requires support (dated where they are versions), and leaves the attack walkthroughs out. The memory skill takes the corrected injection, strings and YARA rules of the memory pack (a string is an observation with provenance, a sound memory-only finding stands without a disk artefact) and names the memory-forensics pack's skills and tools only conditionally.

Each skill's `tools:` and `requires_host:` list exactly what its body names, a name that belongs to a pack this one cannot depend on is in `mentions:` (`mem_profile`, `mem_fs`, `pwsh`), and the skills whose tools can reach a secret carry a Sensitive output line (a job with `secret_output: true`; locations, kinds and lengths, never a value, a fragment or a hash). Where the pack provides no reader, the skill says so instead of implying one: no UserAssist, BAM, ShimCache, SRUM-join, SAM, WMI-repository, bitmap-cache or `$LogFile` decoder, no replay of registry transaction logs, no assembler for split script blocks, no adapter from carved records into a rule engine. Facts that no repository file or report sources (the Amcache hashed range, the availability and retention of several logs and keys, shell-item layouts of later extension versions) are left out or worded as "check the build". `skills/INDEX.md` is regenerated with the new titles. Two goal templates' introductions no longer say that `execution/overview` ranks artefacts or that `artifacts/shell` proves what was opened; their questions and checks are untouched. Pack version 1.5.0, resealed; `unreferenced_ok` records why `pffexport`, `impacket` and `dpapick3` are declared and named by no skill, the last two pending the owner's decision on credential tooling (their entries are unchanged). The 68 lines of the golden list `tests/pack-links.pending` that belonged to this pack are deleted: the pack has no unlisted or unused names left.
