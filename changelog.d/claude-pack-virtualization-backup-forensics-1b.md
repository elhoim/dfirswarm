### Added: the virtualization-backup-forensics pack, second part: the router, seven more skills and the goal template

The skills and the goal template of the pack whose tools came in the previous change. The pack now carries ten skills (a router, a method leaf and eight decision-rule leaves, the largest 755 tokens, none over the budget, no `needs` chain over 1,900) and one goal template.

- **`router/start`** says which note answers which question. **`evidence/boundaries`** names the planes of virtualization evidence (guest, VM files, hypervisor host, management server, backup server, repository), says that each has its own clock and administrators, and sets the handling rules: work from hashed copies, read natively, never power on, register, import, consolidate, commit, rebase or restore from an evidence VM or backup, and treat a credential in a configuration as an indicator and not a login.
- **`control-plane/chronology`** (one clock per source; a task has several times; a guest clock is the guest's; an offset measured once does not hold across a restore) and **`hypervisor/activity`** (the five things to ask of a management record; which planes could have recorded an operation; a bounded negative) name what to do with `backup_audit_normalise`'s time fields and with a declared mapping for products it has no reader for.
- **`snapshots/markers`** reads the candidates `snapshot_chain_check` lists (an unclean-close flag, a dirty bit, a log GUID, a leftover delta, a running disk that is a delta with no snapshot listed, a lock artefact, a sparse extent no descriptor names) and the corroboration each needs; none is a verdict.
- **`backup/deletion-vs-loss`** keeps four states apart for every object (requested, reported, observed, verified loss) and says which evidence shows each; **`recovery/survivability`** gives the five tests a copy must pass before it is called restorable (it exists, its chain is whole, its bytes are readable and intact, the key or credential to use it is held apart from what was damaged, a restore was tested) and the five positions an object can end in.
- **The goal template `what-recovery-survives`** asks eight questions, ends with `Recovery position: <state> - <object>` lines in answer 7, and its acceptance check binds that phrase and one of the five states to answer 7's own section (a pattern for the bare words anywhere in the report would not).
- **Tests.** `tests/pack-virtualization-skills.test.ts` (size and shape rules, the router, the second-level pointer, the names and fields a skill teaches against the tools' source, no version fact, no verdict from a marker, `secret_output: true` where a tool can reach a secret) and `tests/pack-virtualization-goal.test.ts` (the check run on small reports). `README.md` and the pack standard's table carry the new counts (10 skills, 3 tools, 1 goal).
