### Added: a written standard for a pack, and a test that holds the links between its skills, tools and programs

`docs/packs.md` has a new section, "Writing a pack that holds up": what a skill says about an artefact (what it shows, what it does not, what must corroborate it, a bounded negative, a dated time and version), how a tool and a skill handle secret material (no hash of a secret anywhere, no characters of a short one, values only in a `secret_output` file, nothing on a command line), the contract every bundled tool holds to (fail loudly, bound and say so, detect the format version, provenance in every record, evidence as hostile input, an honest manifest, a regression test with an independent fixture), pinned requirements, and the links between skills, tools and programs.

`tests/pack-links.test.sh` checks those links over every pack and its dependency closure. A skill whose body names a tool, program or Python package that only a pack outside its closure carries, a `tools:` or `requires_host:` entry the body never names, and a tool or program the body names that the front matter does not list are bugs and fail the suite. Three more rules are reports for now: every bundled tool is named by a skill of its pack or of a pack that depends on it; every program in `requires/host.json` and package in `requires/python.txt` is named by such a skill or listed, with a reason, in the new optional `unreferenced_ok: [{name, why}]` of `pack.json`; every skill names something of its own pack. `PACK_LINKS_STRICT=1` turns the reports into failures. Packs that do not yet hold to the bug checks are listed in `tests/pack-links.pending`, which the suite counts rather than fails on and which fails once a listed pack is clean, so it only shrinks. Names are matched as whole words; the suite's header says where that is wrong (a program named like an ordinary word, `log`, `make`, `strings`, counts only as a command in code). The suite also builds small packs with one defect each to prove every rule catches it, and seals a pack carrying `unreferenced_ok`. No pack's files change.
