### Changed: the macOS and Linux packs' skills are split to the size budget, and two chain findings of the cloud and mobile packs are gone

The skill budget split pass (docs/packs.md, "Skill size") for the macOS and Linux packs. Every skill of those two packs that was over a budget (a leaf over 1,000 tokens, a `needs` chain over 1,900 tokens or three deep, an index entry over 40 tokens) is now a decision-rule leaf of at most 800 tokens plus second-level leaves its parent points to with "only if ...", and the `needs` edges that only existed for a pointer (to the base pack's `evidence/imaging`, `evidence/verify`, `filesystem/carving`, and between a pack's own skills) are prose pointers. Every old skill id stays alive; the review's rules (what a record shows, what it does not show, what to record, the sensitive-output boundary, bounded negatives, the tools' `status` and field wording) were moved, not cut.

- **macOS** (1.2.0 to 1.3.0): eight skills become seventeen. New second-level leaves: `triage/clocks-hardware`, `accounts/attribution-remote`, `artifacts/plists-archives-dates`, `artifacts/fsevents-dating`, `artifacts/knowledgec-fields-time`, `filesystem/apfs-recovery`, `filesystem/apfs-encryption`, `logs/unified-readers`, `persistence/login-items-quieter`. The largest leaf is 799 tokens. `filesystem/apfs-recovery` and `filesystem/apfs-encryption` name no tool of the pack and are listed in `method_only`.
- **Linux** (1.3.0 to 1.4.0): ten skills become twenty-five (fifteen second-level leaves, for example `logs/auth-lines`, `logs/auth-time`, `persistence/cron-timers`, `filesystem/storage-lvm-luks`); the goals `server-compromise.md` and `what-was-scheduled.md` name the new leaves where a question moved. The largest leaf is 791 tokens. Five leaves that name no tool of the pack are listed in `method_only`.
- **Cloud** (1.3.2 to 1.3.3): `logs/what-exists` no longer `needs` the base pack's `evidence/verify` (it is an "only if" pointer in the body), which brings the eight provider and identity skills under the chain budget; three index entries are shortened.
- **Mobile** (1.4.1 to 1.4.2): `apps/fragments` is reached from `apps/databases` by its pointer and no longer by `needs`.

`tests/skill-budgets.golden` loses the 45 lines these packs had after the base pack's own split (12 macOS, 21 Linux, 11 cloud, 1 mobile). New suites: `tests/pack-macos-skills.test.ts` and `tests/pack-linux-skills.test.ts` (budgets, shape, pointers, the tool fields the skills name, the rules the split kept); the cloud and mobile skills suites gain a chain check.
