### Fixed: virtualization-backup-forensics pack: random letters in a t10. or iqn. id, key flags, a one-class value after a placeholder, names that end in a secret's name, and the 2D of a drive id

Six follow-ups to the way out the three tools share (the block that `virtualization_inventory`, `snapshot_chain_check` and `backup_audit_normalise` carry byte for byte). Unless said otherwise the numbers below are the tools' own functions run on fresh random plants, 1,000 per cell, counted and never listed; a plant counts when the whole value or 12 consecutive characters of it reach the output. "Before" is the pack as it stood, "after" this change. The end-to-end figures are 300 plants per shape planted in the names, paths and free-text slots of all three tools (a VM's name, annotation, guest OS and disk name, a libvirt pool, a Proxmox VM's `lock`, a storage's `server` and `username`, the actor, host, job and result of an audit record), in and out of a job.

- **One case of random letters in a `t10.` or `iqn.` id is no longer readable.** In free text the id around a serial made the run look like words: 20, 24 and 30 small letters as the serial of `t10.ATA_____Model_X_____<serial>` were shown, after the seal and the second check, in 100%, 100% and 99.8% of plants, and now in 0.5%, 0.1% and 0.0% (`t10.AB_<serial>_SN1234` 100, 99.7, 99.9% to 0.9, 0.1, 0.0%; an iqn. target of small letters 7.8, 3.8, 1.9% to 1.0, 0.2, 0.0%). An id in free text is judged now by the same test as in a name (a serial of more than 24 characters, a piece that is random text, a run of 16 or more letters of one case that is not words: fewer than two of the pairs of letters words rarely hold, a vowel in one letter of five, no run of five consonants), and the second check reads such an id as the text it is and finds it alone (silent on 100% of these serials of small letters or capitals before, on 2.4, 0.4 and 0.0% of 20, 24 and 30 characters now). The same gap was open in names and paths: a serial of 30 small letters was shown in 99.8 to 100% of plants (the test did not see an id whose last piece is longer than 24 characters) and is shown in 0.0% now; 20 and 24 small letters in a name 7.5 and 4.1% to 0.5 and 0.1%, in a path 6.0 and 2.9% to 0.5 and 0.1%. End to end 24 small letters as a model were whole in 300 of 300 plants in the inventory and the audit and 15 of 300 in the chain tool, 30 in 300 of 300 in all three, an iqn. target of 24 in 13 of 300 in all three; all 0 of 300 now. Not changed, and listed: a serial of small letters and digits, capitals and digits or both cases has the rates of the previous change (24 characters in a name: small letters and digits 4.1%, capitals and digits 4.9%, both cases 0.4%), and the second check alone is silent on those in free text.
- **A secret's name followed by a short tag, a counter, a date or a file extension is a name again.** The word after a secret's name was judged as a word or a value; `pwd-srv01`, `token-db02`, `Token-20240312`, `Password-000001.vmdk`, `Password.vhdx` and `Secret.nvram` were withheld, and the `D2024` that Veeam glues between a job name and its time stamp (`Password ManagerD2024-...`) made the last word of the job name a value. The word is a name now when it is four characters or fewer of one case (a capital first, digits anywhere), a counter of up to six digits, a date of eight digits or a time stamp of fourteen, 2 to 5 letters of one case and 1 to 4 digits (`srv01`, `db02`, `pg14`), the extension of a file after a dot (72 are listed: `vmdk`, `vhdx`, `qcow2`, `vmsn`, `nvram`, `json`, `vbk` and the rest) or a job name before `D2024`; none of these when `+` or `=` follows it, and with a `/` after it only the four-character one of one case (the value goes on in the alphabet of a key: `password uNFQ/SK1OXbPqJ` is withheld). The words that carry a pair of letters English words rarely hold (`vmware`, `vcenter`, `proxmox`, `sqlserver`, `update`, `upgrade` and about fifty more product and service words) are read as words. Measured on 44 secret names x 5 separators (none, `-`, `_`, space, `.`) x followers, in a name and in a path: 83 dictionary words 0.08% withheld before and 2.27% after the previous change, 0.08% now; 39 host, date and version tags (`srv01`, `svc02`, `dc02`, `pg14`, `cifs`, `vsan`, `ntnx`, `7d4f`, `z3r0`, `p4ss`, `A1B2`, `20191105`, `20240312`, `000001`, `ctk` ...) 1.02%, 56.22%, 3.09% (all of the difference is one token, `x9y8z7`, six characters of letters and digits in turn, which stays a value; 10 leet-spelt words such as `adm1n` and `b4ckup` stay values too); 114 hand-picked names (`Password-Manager-VM`, `token-ring`, `pwd-srv01`, `Okta-Auth-Proxy`, `Password.vhdx`, `Password-000001-delta.vmdk` ...) x 8 contexts 10.4%, 26.6%, 10.4%; a name that ends in a secret's name before `.vmdk`, `.vhdx`, `.qcow2`, `.vmsn`, `.vmsd`, `.nvram` and 14 other extensions, 900 cells, 0.0%, 70.0%, 0.0%; the same job names with Veeam time stamps, 22,400 cells, 55.0%, 85.0%, 55.0% (the rate before the previous change: an older rule, a secret's name beside a long piece of mixed case and digits, still withholds the glued form); 24,000 realistic ESXi, Hyper-V, libvirt, Proxmox, Veeam and vSAN paths with a secret's name in them 21.4%, 35.6%, 16.4%. What must stay withheld stays: a flag with its value (`-password x`, `--token x`, `set password x` and the rest of the list) 0 of 315,000 plants per field kind (name, path, state; 15 forms x 3 lengths x 7 alphabets); a secret's name, a separator and a value of 8, 14 or 24 characters 3,701 of 693,000 plants (0.53%, 3,671 before; 27 of the 30 more are 8 digits that read as a date or 14 that read as a time stamp, 0.1% of random digits, as `Token-20240312` must be shown); the worst cell is 8 small letters, 9.6% as before. Not recognised, and listed: a value of 4 characters or fewer of one case after a secret's name, a tag-shaped value of 5 to 7 characters (`srv01`) and a date.
- **A placeholder followed after a space by a value of one class hides the value.** `password=<redacted> x` kept the placeholder unless the word after it had a digit with a letter or a small letter before a capital, so 8 or 14 small letters, capitals or digits were shown in 1,000 of 1,000 plants, and the second check was silent. Now a word after a placeholder is part of the value when it is 8 or more digits, or 8 or more letters of one case that are not a word (a pair of letters that fewer than 50 of 236,000 words hold, or four consonants in a row), as it was when it mixed letters and digits; a word that reads as a word (`<redacted> successfully`) leaves the placeholder as it is. Seal alone, 3,000 plants per class and length over three placeholder forms: digits 8 and 14 characters 3,000 and 3,000 of 3,000 before, 0 and 0 after; small letters 3,000, 2,998 and 117 at 8, 14 and 24 characters before and 223 (7.4%), 37 (1.2%) and 1 after; capitals 3,000, 3,000 and 108 before, 213 (7.1%), 22 and 0 after; letters and digits 217, 33 and 0 before, 15, 0 and 0 after. The second check alone is silent on 3,000 of 3,000 digits and one-case letters at 8 and 14 characters before and on 0 of 3,000 digits and 244 (8.1%) and 23 small letters (235 and 18 capitals) after. End to end: 8 and 14 digits whole in 300 of 300 plants in the inventory and the audit before, 0 after; 14 small letters 300 before, 1 after; 8 small letters and 8 capitals 300 before, 25 and 23 after (8 letters of one case that happen to read as a word stay shown, 7 to 8%).
- **Every flag that takes a key or a secret hides its value.** `--secret-key x`, `--master-key x`, `--shared-key x`, `--signing-key x`, `--encryption-key x`, `--ssh-key x`, `--secret-access-key x`, `--sshKey x`, `-master_key x` and `-key x` were shown in 100% of plants at 8 characters (48% of 1,008,000 plants over 12 forms x 4 fields x 3 lengths x 7 alphabets); 0 of 1,008,000 now in a name, a path, a state field and free text, in the seal alone, and the second check alone finds all of them in free text (20,011 of 25,200 raw lines it was silent on before, 0 after). The flag is any word that ends in `-key`, `_key` or `Key`, is `key` or holds `secret`, with up to four words before it (`--db-password x`, `--admin-password x` too), after one dash or two, `=` or a space; `--key-file x`, `--keyboard x`, `-keep-alive x`, `--key-size x`, `--keyring-backend x`, `--monkey x` and `--hotkey x` are not one. Of 69 realistic command lines and log lines (qemu, ssh, curl, openssl, gpg, vzdump, pvesm, virsh, govc, esxcli, kubectl, ansible, aws, restic and others) 2 change, both the path of a key file given to `--key` or `--private-key`. End to end 7 flags x 300 plants of 8 characters, in all three tools, in and out of a job: 300 of 300 before, 0 after. The second check's own list of flags is the same as the seal's now and takes a tab or several spaces after the flag. Not recognised, and listed: `-p x` and `--pw x`.
- **A `2D` in a t10. piece is judged.** ESXi writes the hyphen of a drive model as `2D`; a piece with `2D` in it was judged in the parts it separates and parts under 16 characters were never judged, so a random piece with `2D` inside was shown in every plant (3,000 of 3,000 at 16, 20 and 24 characters of capitals and digits that the token test calls random). A piece with `2D` is plain now only in the forms drives carry: a maker's 2 to 4 letters, the digits of a model and blocks of letters and digits, then one or two `2D` and a part of 2 to 10 characters (`WD40EFRX2D68N32N0`, `ST4000DM0042D2CV104`, `MZ7LN256HCHP2D000L2`), or a maker's two letters, `2D` and a serial or the rest of a model (`WD2DWCC7K1234567`, `MZ2D7LN256HCHP2D000L2`), up to 21 characters; any other piece with `2D` is judged whole. Those random pieces are shown in 870 of 3,000 at 16, 426 at 20 and 0 at 24 characters, the forms matching 29%, 14% and none of them; of 6,000 random pieces of capitals and digits the ones with `2D` that were shown fell from 60 to 16 at 16 characters, 74 to 18 at 20 and 42 to 1 at 24. Eleven real model and serial forms from four makers (WD, Seagate, Samsung, HGST) are shown as before.
- **A padded key with a `/`: the figure restated.** The previous change said a 12-character piece of a padded 16- or 20-byte base64 key showed in at most 2 of 1,000 plants in the worst cell; that is the figure for a `/` at a random place. With the `/` forced to each place of the key (20 places for 16 bytes, 25 for 20, and a random place, in 6 contexts: `snap (KEY)`, `{KEY}`, `'KEY'`, `local:KEY.qcow2`, `[ds 1] KEY`, `nfs:KEY`; 1,000 plants per cell, 282 cells) the worst cell shows a piece of 12 characters in 4 of 1,000 (a 16-byte key in `snap (KEY)` with the `/` at the 12th place), 100 of 282,000 plants (0.04%) over all cells, and no whole key in any. The tools' descriptions and the boundaries note say 0.4% in the worst place and 0.04% over all places, and "at most 4 in 1,000".
- **Reading time.** The patterns and functions this change adds or touches (the flag-name pattern in three places, the placeholder tail, the t10./iqn. pattern for free text, the 2D form, the tag, date and extension rules, the second check's id reader) were scanned at 4,096, 32,768 and, as functions, 1,048,576 characters with 56 prefixes (flags, placeholders, `t10.`, `iqn.`, `WD2D`, secret names, `D2024`, brackets, invisible characters) and 44 fillers, each as a prefix and a filler run and as a repeated unit, with eleven scans running at once on a 16-core host (the figures are upper bounds): the 26 touched patterns, 256,256 calls at 4,096 characters (worst 0.003 s) and 128,128 at 32,768 (0.028 s); the calls the tools make (the seal for a name, a path, an identifier, a state field and free text, and the second check) in the inventory 29,568 at 4,096 (0.113 s) and 14,784 at 32,768 (0.65 s, `_suspect` on a run of `:`, as before), in each of the other two tools 14,784 at 4,096 (0.105 s) and 7,392 at 32,768 (0.84 s); 13 functions, 64,064 calls at 4,096 (0.090 s), 32,032 at 32,768 (0.68 s) and 1,612 at 1,048,576 characters, none over 5 s with the others running and the slowest, the name grammar with its length limit off, 2.1 to 2.4 s alone and linear (0.26 to 0.31, 1.05 to 1.2 and 2.1 to 2.4 s at 128 KiB, 512 KiB and 1 MiB, the same as before this change). Nine new shapes are in the linear-time test of the way out.
- **Differential.** 30,000 random cases (45% random values of 8 to 40 characters in 9 alphabets and 55% the shapes of the earlier changes, the key flags, the placeholders, the `t10.`, `iqn.`, `vsan:`, `naa.` and `eui.` forms, prefixed tokens and base64 keys; in a name, a path, a state field, an identifier, a volume and free text through the seal and the second check) run through the pack before and after this change: after it no case shows more of the planted value (12 consecutive characters, or the whole of a shorter one) than before (0 of 30,000), and 1,340 show less; against the pack before the previous change the only cases that show more are a `vsan:` id of 16 hexadecimal digits next to 16 more (24 of 30,000), the stated cost of reading that datastore id.
- **Tests.** `tests/pack-virtualization-followups.test.ts`: 11 tests, 8 of which fail on the pack before this change (at least one for each item but the last) and all of which hold on this one: the serials and targets of small letters and capitals in free text by the seal and by the second check alone, the fixed example of 24 small letters, the names, paths and state fields that must be shown and those that must not, the reading of the word after a secret's name, random values of 8, 14 and 24 characters after every secret name and separator, the one-class values after a placeholder in the seal and in the second check alone, 15 key-flag forms in four fields and in the second check alone, the flags that only look like one, 300 random pieces with `2D` and the real model and serial forms, and an end-to-end run of the flags, the placeholders and the t10./iqn. ids through the free-text slots of the three tools in and out of a job. `tests/pack-virtualization-boundary.test.ts` has nine more shapes in its linear-time test.
- **Docs.** The three tools' descriptions name the flags and the placeholder rule and say 0.4% and 0.04% for the padded key; `evidence/boundaries` says that a value of 7 characters or fewer after a secret's name is not recognised, names `--secret-key x` and the placeholder rule, and says 4 in 1,000 (736 to 768 of the 800 tokens a leaf may use).
