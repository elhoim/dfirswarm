### Fixed: the licence notices say whose each piece is, and every case names its source

`COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md` said that every line of the repository was Halil Öztürkci's. That stopped being true when the fonts, the AD1 test image from Fox-IT's dissect.evidence and the task text quoted from public challenges came in. It now says he holds the copyright in what he wrote, that contributions arrive under `CLA.md`, and that the third-party material NOTICE lists stays under its own authors' terms and is not part of a commercial licence.

- **NOTICE.** The AD1 test image's paragraph gives its copyright holder (Fox-IT, part of NCC Group Plc, made by its Dissect Team) and the licence its `pyproject.toml` declares (AGPL-3.0-or-later); `tests/fixtures/ad1/README.md` says the same. A new paragraph says that the memory-forensics and windows-forensics packs name Volatility 3, which the repository does not include, and links the text of the Volatility Software License 1.0.
- **The image NOTICE.** `vol`'s line in an image's `/etc/dfirswarm/NOTICE` carried the licence's name only; it now carries the link to its text too (the memory-forensics and windows-forensics packs).
- **`SOURCE.md` per case.** Every case folder under `docs/use-cases/` has one: the challenge's author, its link, the licence its source states (`unknown, see link` for all seventeen: none of the pages read names one), the terms the source does state and when they were checked. `tests/use-case-sources.test.ts` fails a case folder without it. No case comes from the DFIR-LABS compilation yet; `docs/use-cases/README.md` says what its `SOURCE.md` names when one does.
