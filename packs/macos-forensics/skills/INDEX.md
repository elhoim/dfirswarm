# Skills in this pack

Fetch a body with `skill("<id>")`. A body may name others; fetch those the same way.

- `accounts/users` Accounts, and who was actually at the keyboard: Attributing an action to a person.
- `artifacts/fsevents` FSEvents, the change log nobody turns off: A file was created, renamed or deleted and you need to prove it happened.
- `artifacts/knowledgec` KnowledgeC and the activity databases: You must say what a person was doing, and when, rather than what exists on disk.
- `artifacts/plists` Property lists, which is to say most of macOS: Any question about configuration, an application's state, or what a user set.
- `filesystem/apfs` APFS, snapshots, and what deletion means now: You need a file's true times, a previous state of the volume, or whether something is recoverable.
- `logs/unified` The unified log: You need what happened on this machine, minute by minute, and syslog is empty.
- `persistence/mechanisms` Where something arranges to run again: You have a payload and need to know how it survives a reboot, or you are sweeping for one.
- `triage/system-profile` Build the system profile before anything else: The first ten minutes of any macOS case.
