# Skills in this pack

Fetch a body with `skill("<id>")`. A body may name others; fetch those the same way.

- `capabilities/mapping` From a file to what it can do: You must say what a sample is for, not just what it contains.
- `documents/macros` Documents, macros and embedded objects: The sample is an Office document, a PDF, an archive or an email attachment.
- `elf/structure` An ELF file, and the Linux equivalents: The sample is a Linux or BSD executable, shared object or core file.
- `pe/structure` A PE file, and what its structure says before any code is read: The sample is a Windows executable, DLL, driver or .NET assembly.
- `rules/yara` Rules, and writing one that will still be right next month: You have a sample and need to find its family, or find it again elsewhere.
- `strings/obfuscated` Strings, including the ones the sample hides: You need addresses, paths, commands or keys out of a binary.
- `triage/quarantine` Working a sample without running it: Before you open anything the evidence produced.
