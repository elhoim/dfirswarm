# Skills in this pack

Fetch a body with `skill("<id>")`. A body may name others; fetch those the same way.

- `aws/cloudtrail` CloudTrail, and the events that are not in it: The tenant is AWS.
- `entra/signins` Sign-ins, and what a successful one does not tell you: You must establish who got in, from where, and how.
- `google/workspace` Google Workspace: The tenant is Google.
- `identity/tokens` Tokens, consents, and why a password reset did not help: The account was recovered but the access continued.
- `logs/what-exists` What the provider actually keeps, and for how long: The evidence is a cloud tenant rather than a machine.
- `m365/unified-audit-log` The Microsoft 365 unified audit log: The tenant is Microsoft and something happened in it.
